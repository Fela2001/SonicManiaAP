**Sonic Mania Plus Archipelago**

**What is Randomized?**

In this Archipelago Randomizer, zone keys, the Chaos Emeralds, random power ups, and brand new collectible item “Phantom Ruby Fragment” will be your items. Act Clears, special stage clears and entrances, and blue sphere clears (and perfects as an option) will be your locations

**Current Goal Condition**

Chaos Emerald Hunt: Find the seven Chaos Emeralds and “x” amount of Phantom Ruby Fragments to unlock Egg Reverie. Defeat Heavy King and Dr. Eggman to finish the game.

**Options**

Blue Spheres Perfects: Adds perfect clears of each blue spheres stage to the locations. Adds 32 more locations in total
